Tennis Marketplace — Privacy Policy

Draft for review. Not legal advice. Have it checked before the App Store submission.

Last updated: 2026-10-27

This policy explains what Tennis Marketplace ("we", "us") collects, why, and what you can do about it. The app is for people in the United States.

1. What we collect

Account

  • Your email address, used to send you a one-time sign-in code. We don't use passwords.
  • A display name (you choose it, 2–40 characters) and, optionally, your US state.

Content you post

  • Listings: title, description, price, condition, gear details, your state, and whether you ship.
  • Listing photos (up to 8 per listing). Before upload the app re-encodes each photo, which removes camera metadata such as GPS location.
  • Messages you send to other users.

Activity you create

  • Favorites, saved searches, and users you block.
  • Reports you file about listings or users. We keep reports without your identity after you delete your account, so we can keep a record of moderation decisions.

Purchases

  • If you subscribe to Pro, Apple handles payment. The app reads your subscription status from Apple (StoreKit) on your device. We don't receive your payment details.

What we don't collect

  • No advertising, analytics or crash-reporting SDKs are in the app.
  • No tracking across other companies' apps or websites.
  • No precise location, contacts, microphone or camera data. You pick photos with the system photo picker.

2. How we use it

  • To run the marketplace: show listings, deliver messages, and let you favorite and save searches.
  • To keep it safe: review reports, remove content, and suspend abusive accounts (see our Terms of Use).
  • To send the sign-in code to your email. We don't send marketing email.

3. Who sees it

  • Other users see your display name, your state, your listings and photos, and the messages you send them. Your email address is never shown to other users.
  • Our service provider Supabase hosts the database, file storage and sign-in for the app. It processes your data on our behalf.
  • Apple handles subscriptions and App Store purchases under its own privacy policy.
  • We don't sell your personal information or share it for advertising. We may disclose data if the law requires it.

4. How long we keep it

We keep your data while your account exists. When you delete your account, we remove your profile, listings, photos, favorites, saved searches, blocks and conversations. Reports you filed stay without your identity.

5. Your choices

  • Edit your display name and state in Profile.
  • Delete your account and its data in Profile → Delete account.
  • Ask for a copy of your data, or a correction, by contacting us (section 8).
  • Residents of California and some other states have extra rights to know, delete and correct their data. We honor these requests whatever your state.

6. Children

The app isn't for children under 13, and you must be 18 or older to sell or message. We don't knowingly collect data from children. If you think we have, contact us and we'll delete it.

7. Security

Data is sent over HTTPS and stored with Supabase. Database access rules limit each user to their own private data. No system is perfectly secure, so we can't guarantee absolute security.

8. Changes and contact

We may update this policy; the date above shows the latest version. For questions or requests (including data copies, corrections and deletion), open an issue at https://github.com/nthakur30/tennis-marketplace-legal/issues. Issues are public, so don't include personal details in them; we'll reply there and arrange anything private from there.
